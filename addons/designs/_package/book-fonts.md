### The book fonts - SIL Open Font License 1.1

The `.ttf` files under `PrismaUI/views/SpellLearning/SpellLearningPanel/themes/fonts/book/` (shared by the Arcane
and Night Grimoire add-ons; installing both writes the same files) are subsets of open fonts under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 (Google Fonts families). Each font's license sits next to it (`<Family>-OFL.txt`), and the
`README.txt` in that folder names every file's source font and license.
