[size=4][b]Heart of Magic Librarian - Toxicology[/b][/size]
[i]Version 1.0.0 by Iwillbewarlock[/i]

Why reserve a carefully brewed poison for coating a weapon when your spells can carry it directly? [b]Heart of Magic Librarian - Toxicology[/b] is an addon for [b]Heart of Magic Librarian[/b] (a fork of Heart of Magic) that brings an alchemist-mage's discipline to your spellcasting. By reading the poison tags written during the Librarian's spell scan, it lets your poison spells deliver the effects of the poisons you carry.

[i]Important: This addon is [b]not[/b] compatible with the original Heart of Magic, as it relies on the poison tags generated by Heart of Magic Librarian's spell scan.[/i]

[size=3][b]How It Works[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Learning the Ability:[/b] Purchase [i]Spell Tome: Toxicology[/i] from Elgrim's Elixirs in Riften (350 gold). Reading the tome teaches the [b]Toxicology[/b] ability, which appears under your Active Effects rather than in your spell list.
[*][b]Delivering Poison by Spell:[/b] With the Toxicology ability active, spells that the Librarian tags as poison carry the effect of your top favorited poison. When such a spell hits a creature, that poison's effect is applied to the target as if you had poisoned it. Helpful spells that the Librarian also tags as "poison" (such as Cure Poison or poison resistance buffs) are skipped.
[*][b]Which Poison Is Chosen:[/b] Among your favorited poisons, the mod selects the first by name—matching the alphabetical order shown in SkyUI's favorites menu. Any real poison item works, including player-made and mod-added poisons.
[*][b]Magicka & Bottle Consumption:[/b] Spell hits themselves use no bottles. Instead, every 100 Magicka you spend casting poison spells uses up one bottle of the poison being copied (the Magicka amount is configurable). When that poison runs out, the next favorited poison automatically takes over; if you have none left, your spells carry no poison.
[*][b]Target Immunity:[/b] After a poison's effect wears off, that creature resists the same poison for a while—by default for as long as the effect lasted, and at least 5 seconds—before it can be poisoned again. Hits during this immunity window do nothing, so a paralysis poison cannot hold a target forever.
[*][b]Alchemy Perks:[/b] Perks that strengthen poisons when you brew them are already baked into your brewed poisons. Perks that add extra doses to weapon poisons do not apply, since no weapon coating is involved.
[*][b]Inventory Icon (Optional):[/b] With [b]Inventory Interface Information Injector (I4)[/b] installed, the poison currently being copied displays a green "flask before a magic circle" icon in the inventory and favorites menus.
[/list]

[size=3][b]Settings[/b][/size]
You can configure the mod via [i]SKSE/Plugins/HeartOfMagic_Toxicology.json[/i]:
[list]
[*][b]enabled[/b] – Enable or disable the mod.
[*][b]showIcon[/b] – Show or hide the I4 icon.
[*][b]magickaPerDose[/b] – Magicka spent casting poison spells per bottle used (default: 100).
[*][b]immunityMultiplier[/b] – Duration multiplier for post-effect immunity (default: 1.0).
[*][b]minImmunitySeconds[/b] – Minimum immunity duration in seconds (default: 5).
[/list]

[size=3][b]Installation & Load Order[/b][/size]
Install with a mod manager and enable [b]HeartOfMagic_Toxicology.esp[/b] (it is ESL-flagged and takes no load order slot). If you use I4, load HeartOfMagic_Toxicology.esp after [b]I4IconAddon.esp[/b] (a new plugin loads last by default).
There is an English main file ([b]HeartOfMagic-Librarian-Toxicology_1.0.0.zip[/b]) and a Korean main file ([b]HeartOfMagic-Librarian-Toxicology_1.0.0_KO.zip[/b]); install only one of them, not both. The Korean file is the same except that the in-game names, effect description, and tome text in HeartOfMagic_Toxicology.esp are in Korean (the tome is "마법책: 독물학" there), with the same plugin file name, install steps, and load order.

[size=3][b]Known Limits[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]The immunity record is kept in memory: after loading a save, every creature can be poisoned once again.
[*]Saves that have already traded with Elgrim's Elixirs will see the tome only after the merchant restocks (about 48 hours).
[*]With InventoryInjectorImproved (I5), the inventory icon can lag; the favorites menu is always right (running [i]i5 purge[/i] in the console refreshes it).
[*]Runes, explosions, and cloak spells are not yet confirmed in game to trigger the poison. Aimed bolts and concentration sprays are confirmed.
[*]Waiting, sleeping, or fast travel moves game time forward and ends immunity early.
[*]NPCs' poison spells do not use this.
[/list]
