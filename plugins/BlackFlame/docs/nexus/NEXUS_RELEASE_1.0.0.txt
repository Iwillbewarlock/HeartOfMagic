EN:
Heart of Magic - Black Flame 1.0.0 is an add-on for Heart of Magic Librarian 1.5.0 or newer. It adds one wearable item, Saeel's Iron Cage Lantern, which you can find on a table on the upper level of Ansilvund Burial Chambers. While you wear it, your own fire spells from Heart of Magic's spell catalog turn into a glowing grey-black flame, fire spells with the fire keyword cost 30% more Magicka, and your fire hits add Black Flame damage equal to 2% of the target's maximum Health over 2 seconds, ignoring resistance. On the first start, wait at the main menu (about 1-2 minutes on the author's PC, longer on slower PCs) while the included helper program makes private grey copies of the fire visuals from your load order. It was tested in game on AE 1.6.1170 only: on other versions, please read the game version table and the known limits first. English and Korean downloads have the same files and FormIDs, so install only one and enable both HeartOfMagic_BlackFlame.esp and HeartOfMagic_BlackFlame_Slots.esp.

KO:
