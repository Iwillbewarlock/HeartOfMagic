2. Changelogs tab - version 1.4.0 (one line per entry)
=====================================================================

Fixed: a tome that teaches a power, lesser power or ability (a mod's teleport power, for one) now teaches it at full strength at once; a save that holds one learned weakened gets it back at full strength on load. Same for a tome spell with no magic school.
Fixed: a spell that only a mod's debug tome teaches (a test cell reached by console) stays out of the tree. Scan and build again.
Fixed: less work behind the game while the panel is closed (reported lower FPS with it closed).
Fixed: scanning freezes the game for much less time - the text work and classification run in the background.
No hotkey, if you like: Settings > Hotkey has a None button, and a warning when you pick a letter key.
More perk trees work with every spell mod: Hydromancy, Atromancy, Hemomancy, Venomancy and Everdamned give their perks to every spell with that element, whatever mod it comes from; Subclasses of Skyrim 2 turns on the Adamant and Ascension adapters.
Cost perks work on more modded tome spells: a spell whose plugin left its half-cost perk empty gets the vanilla one for its school and tier.
Better spell tags: stagger and knockback spells are force; modded poison, sun, necrotic, disease and wind damage recognised; speed, carry weight and skill buffs are strengthen or curse; mod teleports are teleport. Absorbs are not curses, daedra summons not soul, paralysis not creature or human, summons, reanimations and blood-named defences not blood.
Zoomed out (the opening view included), at most 50 spell names are drawn and they have no outline; the selected and learning ones always show, the rest nearest the middle of the screen.
Smaller hitch on the first zoom-in: the spell names' letters are prepared while the panel sits still (most noticeable with Korean, Chinese or Japanese names).
A lighter idle panel: the stars move every second frame, the heart no longer repaints the whole tree, and a tree change is painted once, not twice.

3. Posts tab - announcement (optional)
=====================================================================

1.4.0 is up.

Thank you for the reports - two of them are fixed here:
- A tome that teaches a power (Homeward Bound from Mirele Bismath Reborn, for one) now teaches it at full strength at once instead of a weakened 20% that never grew. If your save already holds such a power, loading it with this version gives it back at full strength with its own name.
- Lower FPS with the panel closed: the panel now does much less behind the game, and scanning freezes the game for much less time.

Also: more perk trees (Hydromancy, Atromancy, Hemomancy, Venomancy, Everdamned) now give their perks to every spell mod, cost perks reach modded spells whose plugin forgot the half-cost perk, better spell tags so the tree groups more spells together, a None hotkey, and a smoother first zoom-in.

After updating: Scan and build the tree again.

If a spell is tagged or grouped wrongly, tell me the mod and the spell name here.
