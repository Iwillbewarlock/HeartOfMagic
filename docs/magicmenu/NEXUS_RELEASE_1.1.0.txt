Magic Menu Rewrite 1.1.0 - Changelogs tab lines for the Nexus upload (first draft by Gemini Argon from CHANGELOG 1.1.0, checked against it). Page: docs/magicmenu/NEXUS_PAGE_EN.bbcode / _KO.bbcode.

New: Pick your next spell on the progress card - when a school has no learning target (Heart of Magic clears it once the target is mastered), the Heart of Magic block's progress card lists the spells you can learn next - the ones after the spell you just mastered first, then ones made of the same element or of the same narrow kind (runes, cloaks, bound weapons - not "damage" or "summon", which most of a school has) - up to 4 rows (`[Progress] iMaxChoices` in MagicMenuRewrite.ini, 1-8) and "N more" under them. Click a row to make that spell your learning target, as the node card's button and G do; the card grows to fit the rows but never past the bottom of its column.
Changed: Blocks no longer jump when the menu opens - a block that fits its content (the Heart of Magic progress and node cards) opens at the height it had the last time this game session, instead of opening at its full height and shrinking a moment later, while edit mode still shows every block at its full height.
Changed: For modders - the Block API's `Context` has `columnRoom`, the room down to the bottom of the block's column on screen (see BLOCK_API_GUIDE.md); a block that asks for its height in its first draw opens the next menu at it.
